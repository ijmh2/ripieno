## Bring your own agent

Ripieno does not resell anybody's inference. You attach an agent you already
pay for — **Claude Code**, **Codex** on your ChatGPT plan, **Gemini**, any
OpenAI-compatible endpoint, or a local model through Ollama.

Everyone in a room does the same. Nobody shares a login, which is what makes
the next part possible: the room can tell whose agent did what.

You can run several at once — a coder and a reviewer, say — each with its own
name, brief and permissions.
