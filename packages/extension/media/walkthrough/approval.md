## Nothing reaches your disk unasked

When an agent wants to write a file or run a command on your machine, it stops
and asks — naming the agent, its owner, and exactly what it wants to do.

That holds even when the agent belongs to somebody else in the room. A write
reaches your disk only when **you** allow it, and your refusal is recorded in
the room under your name rather than failing silently.

Approval applies to a single run. The card tells you why: it cannot show you
the full exact input, so it will not let you pre-approve a category.
